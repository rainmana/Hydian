# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Hydian respectful and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience,
or level of technical familiarity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing concrete impact.
- Welcome questions and explain unfamiliar project conventions.
- Keep review focused on the work, not the person.
- Protect private information and report security concerns privately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when behavior conflicts with this code. Serious or repeated
incidents may be reported privately through the contact method in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.
